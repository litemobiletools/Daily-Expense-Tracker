Daily Expense Tracker – Manage Your Money Smarter

Take control of your money with Daily Expense Tracker, a simple and convenient app for recording daily expenses, managing your budget, and understanding your spending habits.

Whether you want to track everyday purchases, monitor monthly expenses, or organize your personal finances, Daily Expense Tracker helps you keep your financial records organized in one place.

KEY FEATURES

Expense Management
- Add, edit, and delete expense records.
- Record daily spending quickly and easily.
- Organize expenses by category.
- Add expense details, notes, and dates.
- View your expense history.

Income Tracking
- Record your income.
- Track money coming in and going out.
- Review your income and expenses together.

Reports and Statistics
- View spending summaries.
- Analyze expenses by category.
- Monitor daily and monthly spending.
- Understand your spending patterns with visual reports and charts.

Budget Management
- Set spending budgets.
- Monitor your expenses against your budget.
- Keep track of your spending limits.
- Make more informed spending decisions.

Date-Based Tracking
- Review expenses by date.
- Filter records by a selected period.
- Check daily, weekly, or monthly summaries, where supported.

Currency Settings
- Configure your preferred currency.
- View financial amounts using the selected currency, where supported.

Google Drive Backup and Restore
- Back up your financial records to Google Drive, if enabled.
- Restore supported backup data when needed.
- Help protect your records when changing devices.

Privacy and Data Control
- Manage your personal expense records.
- Use available backup settings to control your backups.
- Review the app's privacy policy to understand how your data is handled.

- Clean and easy-to-use interface.
