FAQ

Common questions

Is Daily Expense Tracker Lite free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. No feature is locked behind an ad. Where your region requires it, you will be asked for ad consent and can change your choice in Settings.

Where is my data stored?

Your transactions are stored on your device. If you choose to enable backup, a copy is saved in a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive.

Do I need a Google account?

No. A Google account is only needed if you want to use the optional Drive backup and restore feature.

Can I use a currency other than USD?

Yes. Open Settings → Currency and regional preferences and choose from many currencies, including BDT, EUR, AED, AUD, BRL, CAD and more.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device. To remove a cloud backup, disconnect Google Drive in the app and delete the app's data from your Google account's Drive settings.
