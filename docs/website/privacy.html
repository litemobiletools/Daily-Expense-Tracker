Daily Expense Tracker Lite

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Daily Expense Tracker Lite (“the app”) is an Android app published by Lite Mobile Tools (“we”, “us”) that helps you record income and expenses. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and which third-party services are involved. The developer can be contacted at mdshahinurislam2m@gmail.com.

1. Information the app stores

The transactions, categories, notes, payment methods, amounts, dates, budgets, savings goals and settings you enter are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this information.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks you to grant permission to store a backup file in a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Only this app can access that folder.

  • Backup is optional. Nothing is uploaded unless you tap “Back up now” or turn on automatic backup.
  • The only permission requested is access to the app’s own hidden application-data folder. The app does not request your name, email address or profile, and does not read your other Drive files.
  • When an automatic backup succeeds and has been verified, older automatic backup files in that hidden folder are removed so only the newest one remains. Backups you create manually are not deleted by the app.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Settings → Backup and restore. Disconnecting does not delete data on your device or backups already in your Drive.

3. Google user data

Daily Expense Tracker Lite’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access to your Google Drive is used only to provide the backup and restore feature you request. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with third parties.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is supported by ads served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app shows a small banner ad on its main screens and, at most once every ten minutes, a full-screen ad when you close the Insights screen. Ads are never shown while you enter or edit a transaction, or during backups, restores or exports.

To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. We do not control this processing; it is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states, the app asks for your consent using Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can review or change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options (shown where your region requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Your transactions, budgets, goals and backups are never given to advertisers or to the ad SDK.

5. Information we do not collect

  • We do not sell your financial records.
  • We do not share your financial records with advertisers or data brokers. Ad providers process limited device and advertising data as described in section 4.
  • The app does not request location permission or access your contacts, messages or photos. Ad services may infer approximate location from your IP address.
  • The app has no analytics and sends no crash or diagnostic reports to us.

6. Exports

The app can export reports as CSV or PDF files. These files are saved only where you choose, and are shared only if you send them somewhere yourself.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. To remove a cloud backup, disconnect Google Drive in the app, then remove the app’s hidden data from your Google Account’s Drive settings (Manage apps). You can also revoke the app’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

If Android’s own system backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the app’s data in your Google account backup. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account, not by this app.

8. Security

Backups are transferred to Google Drive over secure (HTTPS) connections provided by Google. The app does not add its own encryption: the database and backups are stored unencrypted in the app’s private storage, and backup files in Drive are protected by your Google account. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

9. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest revision. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact
